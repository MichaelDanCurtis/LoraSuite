# Third-party software and assets

LoraSuite's Apache-2.0 license applies to its original source code. Third-party components retain their own licenses and notices. The npm, Python and Cargo dependency manifests and lockfiles identify the versions used by a build.

Major dependencies include React, Vite, Vitest, Tauri and its plugins, Lucide, FastAPI, Pydantic, NumPy, Pillow, OpenCV, Uvicorn, boto3, sherpa-onnx, gallery-dl and yt-dlp. Inspect each distribution's license before redistributing a bundled installer. PyInstaller has separate licensing terms and a bootloader exception.

FFmpeg is an external runtime dependency; its licensing depends on the installed build. ComfyUI, LoRA Manager, RunPod, RunComfy, social platforms and model hosts are independent projects or services. Their names identify integrations and do not imply endorsement.

No training dataset, personal media, pretrained identity model or generative-model weights are included in the public source/companion release. Downloads can have additional use, redistribution, attribution or access restrictions. Check the specific upstream model's license before use or redistribution.

Signed desktop installers require a build-specific dependency/notice review before publication. The source preview does not claim that every third-party runtime is bundled or redistributable under Apache-2.0.
